## Problem
Large LiDAR datasets needed turning into usable terrain, canopy and volume information.

## Approach
I built a repeatable process covering tiling, classification, DTM/DSM/CHM generation, tree detection, segmentation and volumetric modelling, so each new dataset goes through the same steps and produces comparable outputs.

## Outcome
Consistent surface and canopy products, plus obstacle limitation surfaces (OLS) for airports, supporting operational planning.
