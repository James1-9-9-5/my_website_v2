# 00 - Project Charter

Status: AGREED (2026-11-08). Changes go through doc 11.

## Purpose
This site is a one-stop "About me" and "My work" page I can send to potential employers.

## Positioning
- **Headline (homepage H1):** I help First Nations communities build and maintain asset management and work order scheduling systems.
- **One-sentence summary:** GIS Specialist at Tapestry Research Group who turns field data, spatial analysis and automation into maintenance and asset systems that communities can run day to day.
- **What I want to be known for:** GIS, asset management, maintenance scheduling, field data collection (Survey123 / Field Maps), LiDAR analysis, spatial data automation, community-centred mapping.

## Audience
Potential employers only (decision D6).

| Audience | What they need | What I want them to do |
|----------|----------------|------------------------|
| Hiring managers | Proof of skill and how I work | Open a case study, download the CV, get in touch |
| Recruiters / HR screeners | A quick scan of roles, skills, location | Download the CV, view LinkedIn |

## Success criteria
- [ ] A visitor understands what I do within 10 seconds
- [ ] At least 3 case studies live at launch, each `cleared` or `redacted` (doc 02)
- [ ] Site scores 90+ on Lighthouse (performance, accessibility, SEO)
- [ ] Zero serious or critical axe issues on every page
- [ ] CV PDF reachable in one click from Home, Experience and Contact

## Scope
**In scope (v1):**
- The 7 routes in doc 01 (Home, Experience, Projects + case studies, Skills & Education, Beyond the Map, Contact, 404)
- 3 case studies: Asset Management Systems, LiDAR Analysis Workflow, Spatial Data Automation
- Downloadable CV PDF
- SEO, accessibility and performance checks (doc 07)
- Deploy to GitHub Pages and cut jamesspooner.org over at launch (D5)

**Out of scope (v1):**
- Dark mode (D3)
- Interactive or embedded web maps, before/after sliders
- Tag filters on Projects
- Contact form (mailto only)
- Any blog, Playbook or Lab section

## Constraints
- Hosting: GitHub Pages (static)
- Budget: domain only (jamesspooner.org already owned)
- Time: launch brought forward to 2026-11-08 (D16); case studies expand after launch
- Content permissions: see doc 08 (client and community work must be cleared or redacted before publishing)
